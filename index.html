Certified. Passionate.
Unforgettable.

Adom Catering Service is a certified catering company in Tarkwa, specialised in all kinds of dishes. We have certified chefs and cooks, and we take orders for all occasions. Our aim is to be the best catering service across the globe — customer service is our hallmark. Our core value is respect.
